A single small architect-designed cabin, photographed as real architectural editorial photography.

THE SHAPE IS THE MOST IMPORTANT INSTRUCTION. Seen from the side the cabin's outline is like a running track or an American football field: two long straight horizontal edges, top and bottom, closed at each end by a STRONG CONTINUOUS CURVE. The corner radius is LARGE, about FORTY-FIVE PERCENT of the cabin's height, so each short end sweeps in one smooth arc from the top edge round to the bottom edge, leaving only a SHORT flat segment at the middle of the end. It is emphatically NOT a rectangle, NOT a box, NOT a shipping container, NOT a trailer, and NOT a rectangle with small rounded corners. It is also NOT a tube and NOT a gel-capsule: the ends are flat faces, not domes. Think of a racetrack outline extruded backwards a short depth.

THE SHELL: one seamless monolithic skin of matte charcoal-black charred timber, perfectly smooth and continuous. No wood grain, no planks, no boards, no battens, no ribbing, no panel seams, no vertical striping. Matte, never glossy. The short end facing the camera on the RIGHT is closed and solid.

THE GLAZING: the entire long side facing the camera is glazed from floor to ceiling in tall narrow panels with slim black frames, with a two-panel sliding glass door at the CENTRE. The glass reads as glass, with faint reflections.

THE INTERIOR IS FULLY VISIBLE THROUGH THE GLASS, warmly lit in deep amber, and its walls are NOT bare. Read left to right: a low platform bed with ivory linen under a rounded archway recessed in the pale oatmeal vault, with two small framed pictures on the wall above it and a black articulated reading lamp; a full-height pale timber partition wall; a compact kitchenette with pale wood fronts, a black gooseneck tap and an open shelf carrying three small ceramic pots; a low pale timber island under a single amber glass globe pendant, with a bowl of fruit and a ceramic vase holding one green branch; one potted fern on the floor beside the island; then two cream boucle armchairs, a small round black side table, and a black cast-iron wood stove with split firewood beneath it. Cream linen curtains are retractable and drawn fully aside to the two ends, never closed across the glass. A slim black cylindrical flue rises through the roof on the RIGHT.

THE DECK: the cabin stands well raised on tall slim black steel stilts above a wide, clean, geometric deck of warm mid-brown STAINED timber boards, never pale raw pine. Clean timber steps descend from the near side onto a low square timber landing platform set on a neat bed of pale crushed gravel, clearly delimited from the surrounding ground: a designed arrival space, not steps dropped onto bare rock. One round wooden barrel hot tub with faint steam stands apart on the open ground a few metres away, one small circular stone fire pit holds a low fire, and a handful of small warm ground lights sit low around the cabin. Generous calm empty space around the building, no clutter against its walls.

CAMERA: eye level, standing back and to the LEFT. The cabin is seen at a clear three-quarter angle, its glazed side receding diagonally from left to right, the rounded closed end reading as a volume in perspective on the right, the deck running toward the lower-left corner. NOT a flat head-on elevation. The cabin sits right of centre on the rule of thirds and spans about sixty percent of the frame width, with open landscape visible beyond both of its ends and above it.

LIGHT AND COLOUR: late afternoon into sunset, sun low and raking from the LEFT through high thin cloud, long soft shadows. The whole image is WARM: tan, amber, ochre, copper, bronze and warm gold. It must NOT be grey, NOT slate, NOT cold, NOT washed out, NOT desaturated, and never pink, magenta or violet. Deep true blacks in the shell. Natural colour grade, absolutely NOT HDR, NOT CGI-looking, NOT a 3D render, NOT illustration: this must read as a real photograph taken on a 35mm lens, with real grain, crisp micro-detail in foliage and rock, and genuine atmospheric depth. Charlevoix, Quebec, in autumn. No people, no other buildings, no roads, no power lines, no signage, no logos, no text. Horizontal 16:9 composition.
